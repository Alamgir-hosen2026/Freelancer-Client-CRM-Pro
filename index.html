Privacy Policy — Freelancer Client CRM Pro

Last updated: 19-07-2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Freelancer Client CRM Pro ("the App"), developed by Alamgir App Studio ("we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the App.

By using Freelancer Client CRM Pro, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

a) Information You Enter Into the App

When you use the App, you may enter the following information yourself:

  • Client details: name, email address, phone number, and notes
  • Project details: title, description, deadline, budget, and status
  • Invoice details: line items, amounts, due dates, and payment status
  • Communication log entries (notes about calls, messages, or emails with your clients)
  • Your business name and logo (for invoice branding)

This information is entered by you and relates to your own clients and business — we do not independently collect or verify it.

b) Automatically Collected Information

  • Device identifier: A unique, randomly generated ID (based on your device's Android ID) is used to keep your data separate from other users' data in our cloud database. This identifier does not directly identify you personally.
  • Subscription/purchase information: When you subscribe through Google Play Billing, Google shares limited purchase status information with the App (such as whether your subscription is active) so the App can unlock its features. We do not receive your payment card details — these are handled entirely by Google Play.

c) Information From Optional Features

  • Google Sign-In / Google Drive Backup: If you choose to connect Google Drive (an optional feature in Settings), the App will access your Google account email address and request permission to read/write a single backup file in your Google Drive's private app-data folder. We do not access any other files in your Google Drive.
  • Notifications: If you allow notification permission, the App uses this only to send you local reminders about your own deadlines and payments — no notification data is sent to us or any third party.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above only to:

  • Provide the App's core features (client, project, invoice, and task management)
  • Sync your data across your own devices via our cloud database (Firebase Firestore)
  • Generate PDF invoices with your chosen business name/logo
  • Send you local reminder notifications for deadlines and payments
  • Back up and restore your data if you choose to connect Google Drive
  • Verify your subscription status through Google Play Billing

We do not sell, rent, or share your client data, project data, or invoice data with any third party for advertising or marketing purposes. Freelancer Client CRM Pro does not display ads.

3. Where Your Data Is Stored

  • On your device: Most of your data is stored locally on your device (using a local database), so the App works fully offline.
  • In the cloud: Your data is also synced to Google Firebase (Firestore), a cloud database service, scoped privately to your device identifier, so it can sync across your own devices and remain safe if you switch phones.
  • Google Drive (optional): If you connect Google Drive, a backup copy of your data (in a single file) is stored in your own Google Drive's private app-data folder, which is not visible in your regular Drive files and is only accessible by this App.

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase (Firestore, used for cloud data sync) — https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Play Billing (for subscription purchases) — https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Sign-In & Google Drive API (for optional backup) — https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, including storing cloud data under a private, non-guessable device identifier. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data remains stored on your device and in our cloud database until you delete it yourself within the App (deleting a client, project, invoice, or task removes it from both local storage and the cloud) or until you uninstall the App. If you would like all of your cloud-stored data deleted entirely, you may contact us using the details below.

7. Children's Privacy

Freelancer Client CRM Pro is a business tool intended for freelancers and professionals. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your Rights

Since the data you enter (clients, projects, invoices) belongs to your own business, you are always in control of it — you can view, edit, or delete any of it directly within the App at any time.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the revised policy.
